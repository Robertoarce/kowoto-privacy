Kowoto Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Kowoto is a multiplication training game for children and families. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

Kowoto does not collect any personal information. The app does not ask you to create an account. It does not collect names, email addresses, or location data. It shows no ads and uses no third-party analytics or tracking.

Game progress

Your scores and settings are stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Microphone

The optional voice-answer feature (a premium feature) uses your device's microphone so you can answer multiplication questions by speaking. Audio is processed on your device and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Children's privacy

Kowoto is designed for children and collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect or store any information from children.

Purchases

If you buy a paid feature, Apple App Store processes the payment. We never see your card or payment details. Apple may share standard reports with us (for example, sales totals), which cannot identify you personally.

Artificial intelligence

Kowoto does not use artificial intelligence. No part of the app generates or processes content with AI. If that ever changes, we will update this policy.

Data from the App Store

Apple may provide us with standard, aggregated usage statistics through App Store Connect (for example, downloads and crash reports). This information cannot identify you personally and is provided by Apple, not collected by the app.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: support@kowoto.app

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date above.
